  6. Your Privacy Rights: Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal information.

  8. Changes to This Policy: Santa's team may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any updated version will be published on this page.
  9. Contact Us: If you have questions about your privacy or your application information, please contact the North Pole elf management team through the contact details provided on this website.

Thank you for trusting Santa and his team with your information. We wish you the very best of luck with your application! Merry Christmas! 🎄
